How the 90 days run

One post every day at the audience's best time. The weekly rhythm never changes, so followers learn what each day brings: Monday teaches the concept, Tuesday is the hands-on lab, Wednesday is the code angle, Thursday is the testing mindset, Friday is community and path, Saturday is the deeper lab, Sunday recaps the week.
