## 🎨 BRANDING SECTION

### Logo Concept
**"The Digital Crescent"**
- A sleek, modern shield or geometric hexagon representing cybersecurity.
- Subtly integrated with a minimalistic Crescent and Star to represent Pakistan.
- **Style:** Flat, sharp edges, tech-inspired. No complex gradients in the base logo. 

### Color Palette
- **Obsidian Black (`#0B0F19`):** Primary background. Deep, professional, and mysterious.
- **Matrix Green (`#00F260`):** Primary accent. Bright, tech-focused, attention-grabbing.
- **Crescent Green (`#004B23`):** Secondary accent. A darker, nationalistic green for depth.
- **Steel Gray (`#828F9C`):** For secondary text and borders.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** For primary body text to ensure maximum readability against dark themes.

### Font Pairing
- **Headings (Bold & Techy):** `Rajdhani` or `Space Grotesk` (gives a strong, cyber/tech authority feel).
- **Body Text (Clean & Readable):** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (highly professional, modern sans-serif).

## 7️⃣ CODE OF CONDUCT

**[HEADING]**
**Heading:** PakSec Nation Rules of Engagement
**Subheadline:** Read carefully before joining the community.

1. **Strictly Ethical:** Any discussion, promotion, or sharing of illegal hacking, cracking, carding, or unauthorized access is strictly prohibited and will result in an instant, permanent ban.
2. **No Illegal Activities:** We do not hack social media accounts, we do not bypass university portals, and we do not engage in cyber-bullying. We are professionals.
3. **Respectful Community:** Treat everyone with respect. No racism, sexism, political debates, or religious arguments. We are united by our passion for technology.
4. **Zero Tolerance for Abuse:** Harassment of any member or trainer will not be tolerated. 
5. **Help & Be Helped:** If you know the answer to a beginner's question, guide them. If you don't know, ask politely. There are no stupid questions in PakSec Nation.

*By joining any of our platforms, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.*
