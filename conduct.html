Rules of Engagement.

Please read carefully before joining the community. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against unethical behavior.

1

Strictly Ethical

Any discussion, promotion, or sharing of illegal hacking, cracking, carding, or unauthorized access is strictly prohibited and will result in an instant, permanent ban.

2

No Illegal Activities

We do not hack social media accounts, we do not bypass university portals, and we do not engage in cyber-bullying. We are professionals.

3

Respectful Community

Treat everyone with respect. No racism, sexism, political debates, or religious arguments. We are united by our passion for cybersecurity.

4

Zero Tolerance for Abuse

Harassment of any member, trainer, or volunteer will not be tolerated. Keep discussions professional and constructive.

5

Help & Be Helped

If you know the answer to a beginner's question, guide them. If you don't know, ask politely. There are no stupid questions in PakSec Nation.

By joining any of our platforms, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
