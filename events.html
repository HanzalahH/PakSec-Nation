What You Will Learn

Masterclass Topic Roadmap

01
Week 1

Networking Fundamentals

Understand how the internet works -- IP addresses, DNS, TCP/IP, ports, and basic network architecture.

02
Week 2

Linux Basics

Get comfortable with the command line. File systems, permissions, basic shell commands, and navigating a Linux terminal.

03
Week 3

Defensive Security Mindset

Think like a defender. Learn about threat models, attack surfaces, and how to approach security problems systematically.

04
Week 4

Introduction to Ethical Hacking

Explore the world of penetration testing. Understand reconnaissance, scanning, and the legal framework around ethical hacking.

05
Week 5

Tools of the Trade

Hands-on with Nmap, Wireshark, Burp Suite, and Metasploit. Learn what each tool does and when to use it.

06+
Week 6 & Beyond

Advanced Topics & Guest Speakers

Dive deeper with CISOs, bug bounty hunters, and industry veterans. Topics rotate based on community demand and speaker availability.
